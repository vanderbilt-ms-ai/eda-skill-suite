# Sentences before and after

From case-study drafts, most from the 2020 polls case. Bracketed parts stand for a case's own numbers.

| Before | Problem | After |
|---|---|---|
| "Not the size of the poll - but where it was taken." | reversal | "Polls of every size missed by about 5 points on average. The error grew with how Republican the state voted: 1.5 points more for every 10 points." |
| "Bigger samples. Same miss. Wrong theory." | rule of three | "The quarter of polls with the smallest samples missed by 5.2 points on average; the quarter with the largest missed by 5.1. **Hypothesis 1 does not hold.**" |
| "The polls were biased against Republicans." | claims beyond the data (bias names a cause) | "National polls overstated Biden's margin: 8.4 points on average, against a result of 4.4." |
| "[Case] set a record." | overclaim | "[Case] had [value], the most of any [case] in [the window pulled]." |
| "2020: +8.4 vs +4.4" | shorthand | "National polls put Biden ahead by 8.4 points on average; he won by 4.4." |
| "Hypothesis 2 partly holds, depending on the measure." | rescuing a failed hypothesis | "[The measure the hypothesis names] was [value], below the [comparison] of [value]. **Hypothesis 2 does not hold.** [The other measure] is a new question." |
| "Hypothesis 3 holds on one of its three parts." | a verdict on a bundle of claims | Split it into three hypotheses before testing, each with its own refutation and its own verdict. |
| "Hypothesis 2 holds: group A scored higher." | a verdict on a difference too small to matter | "Group A scored 0.3 points higher on a 100-point scale; the hypothesis set 5 points as the smallest difference that matters for the decision. **Hypothesis 2 does not hold.**" |
| "What actually went wrong with the 2020 polls?" | question broader than the observation | "National polls put Biden ahead by 8.4 points on average; he won by 4.4. Why were the 2020 polls off?" |
| "abs_error_pts is about 5 for every size_group." | variable names in a finding | "Polls of every size missed by about 5 points on average." |
| "Republican states caused the polling error." | causal claim from a correlation | "The more Republican a state voted, the more its polls overstated Biden: 1.5 points more for every 10 points." |
| "It is worth noting that the data tells an interesting story here." | filler, slogan | (delete; state the finding) |
| "Sample size doesn't really seem to matter much." | no number, no verdict | "The smallest quarter of polls missed by 5.2 points, the largest by 5.1. **Hypothesis 1 does not hold.**" |
| "The flag is blank, so it did not happen." | blank read as absence | "The flag is blank in every year, including years when other records show the event happened; it was not recorded." |
| "0.834 of flagged schools" next to "93.6 percent correct" | shares written two ways | Write every share the same way in one document, prose and axes alike: "83.4 percent of the flagged schools", and axes in percent. |
